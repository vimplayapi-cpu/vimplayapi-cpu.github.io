How delivery works

FROM CONCEPTTO LIVE SIGNAL.

A studio is not a room with cameras in it. It is a planned environment, an integrated technical path and a trained team, delivered as one piece of work.

  1. 01

    Plan

    Requirements, output format, operating model and constraints established before anything is drawn.

  2. 02

    Design

    Spatial, set and technical design developed together so the environment works on camera and in operation.

  3. 03

    Build

    Set construction, equipment integration and technical infrastructure installed and commissioned.

  4. 04

    Connect

    Encoding, streaming infrastructure, monitoring and redundancy brought into the live signal path.

  5. 05

    Train

    Production and technical teams trained on the environment, its systems and its workflows.

  6. 06

    Operate

    Live operation, with ongoing technical support and quality-control procedures in place.
