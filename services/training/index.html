Training & Operations

Onboarding, technical training, operational procedures and quality control.

What this covers

In practice

Training and operations covers how the environment is run once it is live. We onboard the team, train them on the technical systems and the production workflows, and put the operational and quality-control procedures in place that make output repeatable.

Included

Scope of delivery

  1. 01

    Onboarding

    Structured onboarding into the environment and its systems.

  2. 02

    Technical training

    Training on the specific technical systems in the environment.

  3. 03

    Operational procedures

    Documented procedures for running the environment day to day.

  4. 04

    Production workflows

    Defined workflows covering the live production cycle.

  5. 05

    Quality-control procedures

    Checks and review procedures that hold output quality.
